How Heddle Cove works

Heddle Cove keeps one short list in the cloud and shows it on the page the moment you open it. Nothing to install and nothing to sign in to.

Lists refresh every time the page loads.

  1. Add an item to the shared list.
  2. Open the Heddle Cove page.
  3. See the five newest items, newest first.

Finished items are marked with a check so you can tell them apart at a glance.

Item stateHow it shows
OpenPlain text
DonePrefixed with a check mark
No itemsA short empty-list message
